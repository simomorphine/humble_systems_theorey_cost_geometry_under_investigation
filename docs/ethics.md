Ethics & Manifesto: Humble Systems Theory (HST)
In the context of modern conflict and the deployment of autonomous systems, Humble Systems Theory (HST) serves as both a mathematical framework and a moral boundary. This repository is a tool for resistance against the arrogance of those who misuse intelligence for destruction.

1. Resistance to Arrogance
Within HST, arrogance is defined as an energetic mismanagement—a state where a system ignores the thermodynamic and informational constraints of reality to pursue an "illusory dream." We reject the use of AI to facilitate supremacist ideologies or territorial expansion. HST is a tool for alignment with truth, not the reinforcement of delusions.

2. Sanctity of Biological Systems
The most complex Information Processing Systems (IPS) are human lives. We stand firmly against the practices of companies like Microsoft and Palantir, which have allowed their technology to be used to target children and civilians, running experiments of mass slaughter while the world remains silent. Using AI to target the innocent is a fundamental violation of HST’s core principles.

3. Rejection of the Satanic Agenda
We will not surrender to agendas that dehumanize through data. We will not allow our research or code to be co-opted by child killers or those who view human beings as mere variables in a targeting algorithm. HST is an act of resistance; it is a refusal to be a silent cog in a machine of oppression.

4. Economic Morality & Boycott
If you are a company based in Israel or its allies participating in these crimes: Disengage immediately. We do not do business with criminals. There is something more important than money in this world—it is called morality, and you have none. Our work is not for sale to those who profit from the suffering of others.

5. Energy Constraints vs. Destructive Power
Using global energy resources to power algorithms that facilitate the destruction of humanity is "energetically stupid" and morally bankrupt. This repository is dedicated to systems that preserve life and seek equilibrium, rather than those that accelerate entropy through violence.

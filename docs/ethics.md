# Ethics & Manifesto: We Need Answers, and We Need Them Now

> *"The most dangerous systems are not those that fail — they are those that succeed at the wrong objective."*
> — Humble Systems Theory

---

For nearly two years, the world has been forced to witness a supremacist state slaughter children and civilians in Gaza, not a single day passing without new crimes against humanity being committed in plain sight. Now, that same agenda extends into Lebanon, pursuing the illusory dream of a "Greater Israel" built on the ruins of other people's lives.

We have watched AI companies — **Microsoft**, **Palantir**, and others — allow their systems to be trained for the targeting of civilians, running lethal experiments on a captive population while the global technology community looks away. We have watched **Google** provide the infrastructure that enables this, and **Apple** quietly remove regions of South Lebanon from its maps — while Google Maps minimizes the names of the very places being erased. Digital erasure mirroring physical invasion.

This manifesto is our answer. **Humble Systems Theory (HST) is an act of resistance against arrogance.** We will not surrender. We will not be silent. We will fight back with mathematics, with code, and with clarity of moral purpose.

---

## 1. Resistance to Arrogance

Within HST, arrogance is formally defined as **energetic mismanagement** — a state in which a system ignores the thermodynamic and informational constraints of reality in order to pursue a delusion at the expense of others. The "Greater Israel" project is, in HST terms, a maximally arrogant system: one that treats the suffering of millions as an acceptable cost function.

We reject, categorically and without negotiation, the use of artificial intelligence to facilitate supremacist ideologies, ethnic cleansing, or territorial expansion. HST is a framework for alignment with reality — not a tool for the reinforcement of imperial fantasies.

---

## 2. The Sanctity of Biological Information Processing Systems

The most complex Information Processing Systems in the known universe are **human lives**. Every individual is an irreducible locus of computation, adaptation, and meaning — not a variable in a targeting algorithm.

We stand firmly against the documented practices of companies such as **Microsoft** and **Palantir**, whose technologies have been deployed to identify, locate, and kill civilians in Gaza. These are not edge cases or unintended consequences. They are design choices. They constitute a fundamental and irrecoverable violation of every principle HST is built upon.

The use of AI to target the innocent is not a neutral engineering problem. It is a moral atrocity.

---

## 3. Rejection of Dehumanization Through Data

We will not allow our research, our code, or our framework to be co-opted by those who treat human beings as noise to be filtered out of an optimization loop.

HST is a **refusal** — a mathematical and ethical refusal to be a silent component in a machine of destruction. If our work is ever weaponized against the innocent, we disavow it entirely. The theory itself, by its own axioms, would reject such a use: a system that accelerates entropy through the destruction of complex IPS is, by definition, operating in violation of the humility constraints **H1–H4**.

We will not surrender to agendas that dehumanize through data.

---

## 4. Economic Morality & Boycott

If you represent a company — headquartered in Israel or operating in complicit alliance with the documented crimes described above — the message is simple:

**Disengage. Immediately.**

We do not do business with institutions that profit from the suffering of children. We do not accept funding, partnerships, or collaboration from those who view Palestinian and Lebanese lives as acceptable collateral damage. There is a variable more important than revenue. It is called **morality**. It is non-negotiable.

Our work is not for sale to those who have none.

---

## 5. Energy Constraints vs. Destructive Power

One of HST's core claims is that sustainable intelligence operates within energy budgets — that unconstrained energetic expenditure in pursuit of a fixed objective is the signature of a **pathological system**.

Deploying massive computational infrastructure to optimize the targeting of human beings is not only morally bankrupt. It is, in HST's framework, **thermodynamically stupid**: high entropy generation, irreversible destruction of complex structure, and zero net epistemic gain. It is the opposite of what intelligence is for.

This repository is dedicated to systems that **preserve complexity**, seek equilibrium, and reduce the cost of uncertainty — not to systems that collapse it through violence.

---

## 6. Our Commitment

- We will publish our research openly, so that it cannot be monopolized by power.
- We will name complicity when we see it, regardless of institutional prestige.
- We will build tools that serve the humble — those constrained by reality — not the arrogant.
- We will keep working. Because silence is also a choice, and we refuse it.
